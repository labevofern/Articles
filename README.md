## 📖 About This Repository

This repository serves as a centralized collection of scientific materials, research data, and computational resources developed within collaborative research activities in the biological sciences.

Its primary purpose is to organize, preserve, and share resources associated with ongoing and completed scientific investigations, particularly in the fields of **biodiversity, evolutionary biology, plant systematics, ecology, biogeography, and integrative biology**.

The repository supports research on plant diversity, with particular emphasis on ferns and lycophytes, while also accommodating broader questions concerning biological variation, evolutionary processes, environmental relationships, and the organization of biodiversity.

## 🔬 Research Scope

The materials gathered here may contribute to different areas of scientific investigation, including:

- **Biodiversity and Taxonomy:** Documentation and investigation of biological diversity and taxonomic relationships.
- **Evolutionary Biology:** Research on evolutionary differentiation, morphological evolution, and diversification.
- **Plant Systematics and Phylogenetics:** Studies addressing evolutionary relationships and species delimitation.
- **Ecology and Biogeography:** Investigations of species distributions, ecological patterns, and environmental relationships.
- **Morphology and Phenotypic Variation:** Research on biological form, structural diversity, and phenotypic differentiation.
- **Integrative Biology:** Approaches combining complementary sources of biological evidence.
- **Computational Biology and Statistics:** Development and application of reproducible analytical methods.

## 💻 Scientific Resources

This repository may include research datasets, computational scripts, statistical workflows, methodological resources, supplementary materials, and documentation associated with scientific projects, theses, dissertations, and manuscripts.

The collection is intended to support **scientific reproducibility, transparency, collaboration, and long-term preservation of research materials**.

Materials may be progressively updated as projects develop, analyses are refined, and scientific publications become available.

## 🌍 Open Science & Collaboration

This repository reflects a commitment to good scientific practices, responsible data sharing, and the dissemination of scientific knowledge.

Research materials are made available according to their respective publication stages, data-sharing requirements, and applicable restrictions.

Contributions, scientific discussions, and potential collaborations are welcome.
